<p>I am currently a postdoctoral researcher working with Eric Gawiser and Saurabh Jha at Rutgers University. I received my Ph.D. in Astrophysical Sciences from Princeton. My research studies the early conditions, composition and evolution of the universe. I use a combination of cosmic microwave background and large-scale structure data together with statistical data analysis to compare how well different models describe our observations.   </p>
